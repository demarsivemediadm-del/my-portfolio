The studio

Creative and technical, in one studio.

Demarsive Media is a digital creative studio in Accra, Ghana, founded and led by Augustine Ackah. He started in IT and moved into design and video, so the studio can take a brand from the first logo sketch to a finished video and a working website.

The goal on every project is the same: a brand that looks trustworthy, tells its story clearly, and gets remembered.
